## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**Does this look AI-generated?** Not at first glance — the Ken Burns photography, Lat/Lon HUD coordinates, grain texture, and restrained navy/paper rhythm show real, deliberate art direction. A discerning viewer would say "good direction, sloppy execution of its own rules," not "obviously AI." But it does not pass a rules-compliance audit clean.

**LLM assessment (Assessment A):**
- **The Mono-Is-Metadata Rule: PASS.** JetBrains Mono is used consistently only for eyebrows/HUD/coordinates, never on headlines or body.
- **The Real-Photo Rule: PASS.** All hero/stats/works/clients imagery is real `next/image` photography; no stock or illustration detected.
- **The One Gradient Rule: VIOLATED.** `Stats.tsx` stacks `text-gradient-brand` on the headline AND on every one of the 4 stat suffixes — 5 simultaneous gradient instances in one viewport, against the rule's own "never more than one element simultaneously in the viewport" language.
- **The No-Synthetic-Shadow Rule: VIOLATED twice.** `About.tsx` and `Equipe.tsx` both add `hover:shadow-[...]` soft-lift shadows on card hover, beyond the one permitted exception (the primary CTA glow).
- **Numbered section markers: broken, not just stylistically risky.** `SectionHeader`'s index prop sequence (01 About, 02–03 Solutions/Estrutura, 04 Obras, 05 Equipe, 06–07 Clientes/Contato) is split across three different routes. On the homepage alone, a visitor sees 04 → 06 → 07 with no 01/02/03/05 ever appearing and no acknowledgment of the gap — this reads as broken bookkeeping, which is worse than the generic "numbered eyebrow" tell the brief warns about, because it's not just a reflex, it's incorrect.
- **Generic icon-chip-above-heading pattern: present 9 times.** `Solutions.tsx` (6 cards), `About.tsx` (3 pillars) — and `Equipe.tsx`'s founder cards reuse the same rounded-square icon motif. For a brand whose differentiator is photographic proof of real work, this is the off-the-shelf SaaS tell the brief explicitly bans, applied three separate times.

## Overall Impression

The visual direction ("The Industrial HUD") is genuinely executed with care — real photography, restrained palette, accessibility-conscious motion handling (`prefers-reduced-motion` implemented broadly). But the site's only conversion mechanism, the contact form, **does not submit anywhere** — it's a fully simulated success state with zero network call. Combined with a homepage that shows proof-of-work and numbers but omits the credibility narrative (licensed engineers, mission/values) the brief says this exact audience weighs heavily before a sales meeting, the single biggest opportunity here isn't visual polish — the polish is mostly already there — it's making sure the page actually does its one job.

## What's Working

1. **Hero's HUD geometry** (`CornerTicks`, Lat/Lon coordinates, "Obra 01 · C.Vale" caption) — a distinctive, on-brief execution of the Industrial HUD concept that differentiates this from generic dark-hero SaaS sites.
2. **`Obras.tsx`'s full-bleed sequential work panels** — strong "one thing at a time" composition; each project breathes and reads as real large-scale work, not a thumbnail grid.
3. **Token and motion discipline in `globals.css`** — the navy/brand/paper scale is restrained and consistent, and `prefers-reduced-motion` is implemented thoroughly across Hero, Stats, Obras, Reveal, and Magnetic.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Contact form does not submit anywhere.**
**Why it matters**: This is the site's only stated conversion mechanism. Every "Enviar mensagem" click shows a fake success state ("Mensagem recebida! Resposta em até 1 dia útil") while the lead is silently lost. A real prospect (a procurement engineer, a municipal official) believes their message was received and may not follow up by phone for days.
**Fix**: Wire `Contato.tsx`'s `handleSubmit` to a real endpoint (Next.js API route + email/CRM webhook, e.g. Resend as the README already suggests), with pending/error states, not just optimistic `setSent(true)`.
**Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

**[P0] Homepage omits the credibility narrative the target audience weighs most.**
**Why it matters**: `app/page.tsx` renders only Navbar/Hero/TrustBar/Obras/Stats/Clientes/Contato. The CREA-licensed founders, mission/vision/values, and full solutions breadth live only on `/sobre`, `/solucoes`, `/equipe` — never on the page a visitor actually converts on. PRODUCT.md's own success criterion is "qualified contact from someone already convinced of technical solidity," but the technical-solidity evidence (licensing, engineering credentials) isn't on the conversion page.
**Fix**: Either surface a condensed founders/credentials block on the homepage ahead of the contact form, or make the multi-page architecture explicit in the nav so visitors don't assume everything is one scroll away.
**Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

**[P1] Section index numbering is broken across pages.**
**Why it matters**: The same numbered-eyebrow pattern means different things on different pages (04→06→07 on the homepage with no explanation), directly violating Consistency & Standards and reading as bookkeeping error rather than brand voice.
**Fix**: Make indices page-local (restart at 01 per page) or drop the cross-page sequence entirely.
**Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify`

**[P1] Contact form has zero custom error states.**
**Why it matters**: For a brand whose entire voice is "técnica, confiável," a bare native-browser validation popup on the one form that matters undercuts the deliberate, engineered feel built everywhere else.
**Fix**: Add inline, HUD-styled error microcopy per field instead of relying on default browser validation.
**Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] Gradient and shadow rules are violated by the system's own components.**
**Why it matters**: `Stats.tsx` shows 5 simultaneous gradient instances in one viewport (headline + 4 suffixes); `About.tsx`/`Equipe.tsx` add soft-lift hover shadows beyond the one permitted CTA exception. Small individually, but this is exactly the kind of drift that turns "deliberate brand system" into "decorative habit."
**Fix**: Limit the gradient to the Stats headline only (render suffixes in plain `brand-300`); replace the two extra hover-shadow instances with the border/background-tint hover pattern `Solutions.tsx` already uses correctly.
**Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`

Separately, no skip-to-content link exists anywhere — a keyboard user must tab through the full nav (5 links + CTA + mobile toggle) on every page load before reaching content.

**Marcos (project-specific — a C.Vale/Copacol-style cooperative procurement engineer evaluating Centra during a break before a board meeting)**: Wants three things fast: proof of work at his scale, who's licensed/accountable, and a way to start a proposal today. When he does fill out the contact form expecting a 1-business-day response, his submission goes nowhere (the P0 form bug), and he won't find out until he follows up by phone days later — by which point "técnica, confiável" has flipped to "their site doesn't even work."
